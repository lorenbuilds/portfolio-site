### Project Overview

Led the successful migration and enhancement of an enterprise-level e-commerce platform for a premium restaurant equipment distributor. The project focused on modernizing their digital presence while implementing advanced sales features to drive conversion rates.

### Key Responsibilities

- **Data Migration**: Successfully transferred GTM data flow to customer events
- **Checkout Enhancement**: Upgraded checkout.liquid to Shopify Plus checkout extensibility
- **Sales Features**: Implemented dynamic countdown flash sales in the announcement bar
- **Cart Optimization**: Developed enhanced cart drawer with smart upsell recommendations
- **UX Improvements**: Resolved indentation issues and refined branding elements

### Technical Highlights

- Seamless integration with Shopify Plus platform
- Implementation of real-time inventory tracking
- Development of custom cart upsell algorithms
- Mobile-responsive design optimization
- Enhanced data tracking and analytics implementation

### Results

The migration and enhancements resulted in:

- Improved checkout completion rates
- Increased average order value through strategic upsells
- Enhanced user experience with streamlined navigation
- Better data insights for business decision-making
