## Figma to Shopify: Custom Home, Product Page, & Mega Menu Development

<div style="margin: 40px 0;"></div>

<b>My role:</b> Frontend Developer for Figma-to-Shopify Transformation

---

In this project, I transformed a client's Figma mockup into a fully branded Shopify homepage, ensuring consistency and alignment with the provided design.
<br>
Key implementations include:

- A custom slider for various section, optimized for both desktop and mobile views
- Tailored their product page to match the mockup's specifications
- Customized the mega menu to include a sliding feature upon hovering over collection links, with full mobile responsiveness
