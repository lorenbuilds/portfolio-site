## Build & Integrate Subscription Portal with Ordergroove (Shopify)

<div style="margin: 40px 0;"></div>

<b>My role:</b> Shopify Developer - Integrate Ordergroove API & Implement Custom Subscription Portal

---

Created a comprehensive e-commerce solution for Lithe and Limber, a premium dog supplement brand focused on joint health and mobility.
<br>
Key features include:

- Implemented branding for the client’s account dashboard based on the provided Figma design
- Automated subscription management system for recurring deliveries
- Interactive product quiz to help customers find the right supplement
- Integration with inventory management and fulfillment systems
- Mobile-responsive design with seamless checkout experience
- Customer dashboard for subscription management and order tracking
- Integrated the Ordergroove API to manage order processing, upcoming shipments, and deactivated subscriptions
